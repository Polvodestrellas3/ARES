Notes from the field

03 / Science stations

An ancient river carried sediment into Jezero’s lake. By investigating the textures and layers that remain, a future crew could study the history of water and whether past environments might have supported microbial life. These sites are based on Perseverance observations; astronaut activities are proposed for this prototype.

Data provenance, assumptions & limitations

$note

Terrain. The geology layer is regional (1:75,000). Slope is the mean inclination between elevation samples about 10 m apart, not a complete hazard map. Overlapping route segments display the steeper sampled slope. The difficulty bands are illustrative. Small obstacles may not be resolved.

Planning. Walking time assumes constant mean speed. Field time is the number of visited sites multiplied by time per site. The recommendation first filters out routes beyond the time budget, then ranks those remaining by the selected priority. It does not model obstacles, atmosphere, radiation, oxygen, battery use or astronaut physiology. No route is certified for real human operations.
